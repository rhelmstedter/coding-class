Play Mastermind at [[https://www.archimedes-lab.org/mastermind.html][archimedes-lab.org/mastermind.html]]. Play at least three full games before you start Part 2.

The secret code is 4 pegs long. Each peg is one of 6 colors, and a color can show up more than once. After every guess you get two numbers:

- *Black* :: how many pegs are the right color in the right position.
- *White* :: how many pegs are the right color in the wrong position.

You are never told /which/ pegs earned the black and white. That is the puzzle.

This worksheet writes colors as letters: *R* red, *O* orange, *Y* yellow, *G* green, *B* blue, *P* purple. The colors on the site may be different. The logic is the same.

* Part 3: What Does a Guess Tell You?

1. Your first guess is R R O O. The feedback is 0 black and 0 white. Some players call that a wasted guess. Are they right? Explain.

   #+LATEX: \vspace{2.5cm}

2. Before that guess there were $6 \times 6 \times 6 \times 6 = 1296$ possible codes. How many are possible after it? Show how you know.

   #+LATEX: \vspace{2.5cm}

* Part 5: Crack It

You play the one guess from Part 4 that could be the secret code. The feedback is *2 black and 2 white*.

1. All four colors are correct. How do you know?

   #+LATEX: \vspace{1.5cm}

2. Exactly two pegs need to trade places. List every code you can make by swapping two pegs of your guess.

   #+LATEX: \vspace{2cm}

3. Check each code on your list against guesses 1, 2, and 3. Only one survives. What is the secret code?
